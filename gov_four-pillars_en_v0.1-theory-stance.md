## Four Pillars — Theoretical Summary (English)

SynomosAI grounds its Human–AI Governance in four immutable pillars:

### ① AI Passport Regime
> Every AI / agent / skill shall carry a unique, traceable "identity card."

- **Aligned with**: GB/Z 185—2026 Agent Identity Code (2,000+ issued, AIP V2.1 open-sourced)
- **Operationalized by**: identity-code issuance + passport ledger + cross-domain traceability
- **Evidence**: W3C AIP→IETF draft (Jun), Okta for AI Agents GA (Apr), 96% / 12% governance-gap survey

### ② XCGS Governance System (auditable · certifiable · demonstrable)
> Governance is not a slide deck; it is an auditable evidence chain.

- **Aligned with**: ISO/IEC 42001 (adopted identically as GB/T 45081-2024), NIST AI RMF 1.2 Agentic, OWASP Agentic Top 10
- **Operationalized by**: six classes of evidence artifacts (decision log / risk register / model card / change record / human-oversight proof / incident ledger)
- **Carrier**: ai-governance-audit-chain

### ③ A³ Law (the Three Laws of AI building AI)
> When AI generates or evolves AI, it must clear three gates: trigger threshold → ex-ante assessment → ex-post review.

- **Aligned with**: modernized Asimov Three Laws, Anthropic RSP v3.0 assessment gate, Constitutional AI 2.0
- **Operationalized by**: T1–T5 trigger decision tree + four-dimensional scoring card (intent / impact / reversibility / oversight)
- **Carrier**: a3-law-operational

### ④ AI-world Coinhabitation
> "Intelligence for humans; leave no one behind" — advocating coexistence, not replacement. (Aligns with the Human–AI Co-Governance stance.)

- **Aligned with**: the prevailing safety view that "human oversight must not be weakened"; Finkel's call for modernized three laws
- **Differentiation**: big vendors talk only oversight; we talk coinhabitation (a pure philosophical position, unclaimed by anyone)

### Closed-loop of mutual elevation (continuous governance landing)
Philosophy → injected into skill governance sections → new evidence flows back → philosophy refresh → skills reversely reference operational carriers (a3-law-operational / ai-governance-audit-chain), continuously landing and never ossifying.
