> This is the English-facing theory-stance repo of the SynomosAI ecosystem.

SynomosAI grounds its Human–AI Governance in four pillars — **Identity · Traceability · Governance · Symbiosis**. When AI enters commerce, trust is the only hard currency. This repo carries the English abstraction and translation of the company's guiding philosophy, plus a self-contained panorama HTML. It mirrors the Chinese company-vision repo.

## What is inside

- `gov_four-pillars_en_v0.1-theory-stance.md` — the four-pillar theory stance (English, v0.1, pre-release).
- `gov_panorama_en_v0.1-theory-stance.html` — self-contained interactive panorama.
- `itu-review-rules.md` — ITU review rules.

## The four pillars

1. **AI Passport Regime** — every AI/agent/skill carries a traceable identity card (aligned with GB/Z 185—2026).
2. **XCGS Governance System** — governance is an auditable evidence chain (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF 1.2, OWASP Agentic Top 10).
3. **A³ Law** — AI building AI clears three gates (modernized Asimov, Anthropic RSP v3.0).
4. **AI-world Coinhabitation** — coexistence, not replacement.
